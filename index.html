What Makes It Stand Out

Found and fixed a critical bug

02 / IS/OOS Discipline
OOS Sharpe exceeds IS by 1.34×
2018–2021 was used for in-sample design only. 2022–2026 was held out completely and never touched during parameter selection. OOS outperforming IS is the opposite of overfitting.
03 / Honest Research Process
15 signals explored, 11 rejected
Every failed experiment is documented with honest OOS results and reasons for rejection. Signals with negative OOS Sharpe (e.g. Opening Drive: −19.94) were cut regardless of IS performance.

Project 02 — Quantitative Research

Quantitative Research & ML

Directed Research Project · May 2026

MISO Day-Ahead LMP Forecasting

Research question: Does adding net load and capacity-aware stress features improve day-ahead electricity price prediction in MISO LRZ4?

Model RMSE ($/MWh) MAE ($/MWh) Directional Accuracy
Prev Hour Baseline 16.90 8.53 36%
Same Hour Yesterday 17.84 6.97 52%
Linear Regression 11.53 4.14 76%
Ridge Regression 11.53 4.14 76%
XGBoost ★ 11.56 4.22 75%
Key Finding
2× improvement over naive baseline
Net load and stress proxy features drove RMSE from ~$17/MWh (naive) to ~$11.5/MWh. Directional accuracy improved from 36% to 76%.
Honest Limitation
Spike periods remain hard to predict
Above $75/MWh, RMSE exceeds $130/MWh across all models. This is a fundamental market property — spikes are driven by stochastic events not captured by pre-spike features.
